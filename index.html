Function-powered artillery · two-player local game
PHASE 1 · SPAWN
Player 1 100 HP
Player 2 100 HP

Spawning both players…

Function model: y = a(x − h)² + k. The projectile is drawn from the launch point along the entered curve; keep the curve aimed toward the other player.

Turn controls

Players spawn first. Then Player 1 takes the opening turn.
Try a = -0.018, h = 100, k = 100. Adjust h and k to aim. Negative a makes an arch that opens downward.
